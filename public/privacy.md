# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-02-06

## 1. Information We Collect
- Discord User ID or Telegram Chat ID
- Alert preferences: cryptocurrency symbol, target price, direction (above/below)
- Timestamp of alert creation

## 2. How We Use Information
- To send you price notifications
- To store and manage your active alerts
- To improve service reliability

## 3. Data Storage
- Data is stored in a private SQLite database.
- Only the service administrators have access.
- Data is retained only while alerts are active.

## 4. Data Sharing
- We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.
- Data is not used for advertising.

## 5. Your Rights
- View your active alerts via bot commands (if implemented).
- Delete all your alerts by messaging the bot: `/deleteall` (if implemented).
- Request data deletion by contacting us.

## 6. Security
- We take reasonable measures to protect your data.
- No system is completely secure; use at your own risk.

## 7. Retention
- Alerts are automatically deleted after they trigger.
- Inactive alerts may be purged after 90 days.

## 8. Changes
- We may update this Privacy Policy. Continued use constitutes acceptance.
